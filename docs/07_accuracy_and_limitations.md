# Accuracy And Limitations

## Satellite/Model Limits

NASA GRACE-FO/GRACE-DA products provide satellite/model signals and percentile context. They do not provide exact groundwater depth at the station or mandal level.

## Scale Mismatch

GRACE-family products operate at much coarser spatial scales than mandal boundaries and individual piezometers. A mandal-level fusion output can use these signals for directional context only.

## Mock Data Limits

The mock APWRIMS-like readings in this repository are realistic fake samples. They are suitable for schema and pipeline validation, not operational groundwater assessment.

## Prototype Boundary Limits

Public GitHub boundary and name datasets may be stale, incomplete, invalid, or inconsistent with official APWRIMS/AWARE administrative records. They are prototype-only until replaced by official APWRIMS/APSAC/RTGS boundaries.

## Official Output Requirement

Official mandal-level results require:

- official APWRIMS/AP government groundwater readings
- official APWRIMS/APSAC/RTGS boundaries
- verified APWRIMS admin IDs or crosswalks where available
