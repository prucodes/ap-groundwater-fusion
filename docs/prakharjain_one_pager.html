The one scientific point — and why it makes the system stronger

No satellite — GRACE-FO included — measures absolute groundwater level (metres below ground). It measures whether the aquifer holds more or less water than its own history: a stress & trend percentile. Absolute levels still require sensors. So rather than replace your sensors, the system fuses them: satellite covers all of AP continuously and shows where & which-way; sensors anchor exact depth where you have them. This is more defensible than satellite-only — and it tells you where to place your next sensors. The product is built around exactly this truth (it labels percentiles, never fake depths). Where a mandal has sensor history, the model also estimates depth in metres — gap-filling missing months and forecasting the next — calibrated to those sensors and validated to ±1.3 m on months it never saw.
