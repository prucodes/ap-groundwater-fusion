GROUNDNUT PROMPT
Use case: scientific-educational with photorealistic-natural finish. Create a production image atlas for a premium interactive agriculture exhibit. PORTRAIT sheet of exactly THREE equal-height landscape frames stacked vertically, NO margins, gutters, text, borders or dividers. Full sheet aspect 16:27; every frame has the same 16:9 crop and fixed straight-on camera. Each frame shows a realistic GROUNDNUT (peanut, Arachis hypogaea) crop and a natural soil cross-section below ground. Groundnut is a LOW growing leafy legume with small oval leaflets in sets of four; do not depict tall maize or beans on poles. A few separate foreground plants across 25%, 50%, 75% width, sunlit Andhra-like South Indian agricultural context, distant fields and palms and soft low rocky hills. Exact ground surface is 56% down each frame. Lower 44% is continuous real granular loam soil exposed as a flat vertical cutaway with natural fine roots, texture and stones, no floating cube or side wall. TOP FRAME initial: small groundnut seedlings, a few pairs of oval green leaflets, delicate emerging roots, NO pods. MIDDLE FRAME mid-season: lush low rounded bushes of many oval leaflets, small inconspicuous yellow flowers, fine branching roots and a few downward pegs entering the soil; only a few tiny young pods underground, not fully harvested nuts. BOTTOM FRAME end-season: mature low groundnut plants with some natural yellowing leaves but still healthy structure, well-developed peanut pods below ground attached by pegs, natural tan rough pod shells and fine branching roots. No pods above ground, no individual shelled peanuts. Botanical proportions must look real. Keep camera, soil level, sunlight, backdrop and exposure identical across the three frames. Documentary photographic realism and high-end museum scientific cutaway, not painted/cartoon, not 3D plastic, not low-poly. Rich but restrained real greens, warm soil, soft blue sky, true leaf texture, fine natural details, bright daylight. No arrows, water graphics, measurements, labels, text, numbers, logos or watermarks. This is an illustrative composite, not a surveyed site.
