# Beneath the Monsoon

The creator introduction for AP Water Intelligence. These captures show the
production static export at 1440 px and 375 px, before entry.

- [Desktop](desktop.png)
- [Phone](phone.png)

The animated vector landscape uses the existing prototype AP silhouette. Its
rain, layers, river strokes and signal points illustrate a water landscape;
they do not claim to be observations or an official geological cross-section.

`PRUTHVI` opens the requested workspace route and is remembered locally. This is
an introduction on the existing public site, not authentication. The app's
creator credit reopens it. Motion can be paused and honours reduced motion.

Creator attribution also appears in shared CSV banners, the evidence JSON pack,
the project brief and the weekly digest. GoatCounter records entry, workspace
opening, download requests and printing without receiving entered text or names.
