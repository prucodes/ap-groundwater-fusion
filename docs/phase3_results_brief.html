The one honest point — and why it makes the system more trustworthy, not less

The reproducible same-month comparison contains 16,097 nearest-site pairs within 20 km and yields 6.12 m MAE, r 0.40. It is a network-comparability diagnostic: wells/sites, aquifers, aggregation and observation protocols differ. It is neither model validation nor a universal uncertainty floor. Model ranges are labelled P10–P90 quantile ranges rather than guaranteed confidence intervals.

5 Candidate data sources — evidence, not a claimed ceiling

To answer "can we get closer, with less sensor dependency?", we rigorously tested every promising open source — including building a full Sentinel-1 InSAR pipeline. The result defines exactly how far open data can take us.

The bottom line — task-specific evidence

The 1.30 m result belongs to temporal nowcasting where lag history exists; the current whole-mandal result is about 4.86 m MAE. Existing experiments do not prove a universal open-data ceiling. Better sensorless performance should be pursued through leakage-safe hydrogeology, terrain, crop/water-use, soil, InSAR and climate features, evaluated by whole-mandal and spatial-block holdouts.
