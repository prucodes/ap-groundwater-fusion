The one honest point — and why it makes the system more trustworthy, not less

When we cross-checked against CGWB’s own independent well network (1,018 AP stations), the two official networks agree only to ~3–6 metres, with CGWB reading ~4 m shallower. This is not a model error — CGWB’s shallow dug wells and APWRIMS’s deeper piezometers measure different aquifer zones, and groundwater varies several metres even between wells a few km apart. The lesson: “groundwater level in metres” is never one exact number — it depends on the well and the aquifer. That is exactly why the engine ships a confidence band on every estimate and labels everything “calibrated to APWRIMS.” We measured the irreducible uncertainty rather than hiding it.

3 What it does — and the forecasting sweet spot

Capabilities today

  • ✓Estimate metres at any mandal with some history, to ~±1.3 m, with a band.
  • ✓Gap-fill missing months where sensors lapse.
  • ✓Forecast a season ahead (3–6 mo) to ~±2–2.5 m — beats naive by ~20%.
  • ✓Auto-refresh weekly from NASA — no manual data feeding.

Honest limits

  • !A mandal with no history at all ≈ ±5 m (worse in hard-rock Rayalaseema).
  • !12-month forecasts don’t beat “same month last year” — we don’t oversell long range.
  • !Calibrated to APWRIMS; absolute truth is network-dependent (see box).
  • !Labelled β / not official throughout.
